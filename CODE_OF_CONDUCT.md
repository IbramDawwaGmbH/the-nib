# Code of conduct

Nib is a small, friendly project. Keep it that way.

- **Be kind.** Disagree with ideas, not people. Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- **Be welcoming.** Newcomers, first issues and "silly" questions are all welcome here.
- **No harassment.** No insults, personal attacks, slurs, sexual content, or unwanted attention, in
  issues, pull requests, commits or anywhere else connected to the project.
- **Keep it on topic.** Issues and reviews are about the code, the art and how he behaves.

## If something goes wrong

Report it privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/IbramDawwaGmbH). Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may edit
or remove comments, commits or issues that break these rules, and may block anyone who keeps
breaking them.
